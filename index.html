About

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering student focused on embedded systems, PCB design, and hardware security. I build real systems from circuit design to firmware integration, with an emphasis on reliability and practical use.

What I work on

Microcontroller development, wireless systems, CAN bus analysis, BLE systems, RF fingerprinting, and secure embedded designs. I prefer problems that start in hardware and end in something tangible.
